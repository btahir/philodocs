# History of Thought Timeline

This is a starter index for the personal philosophy wiki. It is intentionally metadata-only:
no doctrine summaries, no major-work summaries, and no interpretation yet.

The goal is to decide the first set of pages worth creating, keep the chronology visible,
and preserve enough tags to support later clustering, linking, and graph views.

## Scope Rules

- Include philosophers, theologians, political theorists, social critics, religious founders,
  literary-intellectual figures, and collective textual traditions when they are major nodes
  in the history of human thought.
- Prefer influence and connective value over encyclopedic completeness.
- Dates are approximate when ancient or contested.
- `P0` means essential coverage for the wiki. `P1` means strong follow-up page.
  `P2` means context node, school page, or optional expansion.
- Suggested future page path: `content/thinkers/{slug}.md`.
